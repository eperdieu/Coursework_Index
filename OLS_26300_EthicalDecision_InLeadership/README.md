# OLS 26300: Ethical Decisions in Leadership

Purdue University Indianapolis, Polytechnic Institute | Fall 2023

## About the Course

A course on ethical theory and how leaders make decisions in real business cases. Work included weekly case discussions, a professional code of ethics review, and a final personal ethics plan.

## Topics

* Ethical frameworks and decision making
* Professional codes of conduct, including the ISSA Code of Ethics
* Ethics in leadership and organizational culture

## Skills

Ethical analysis, case discussion, applying professional codes to real situations
