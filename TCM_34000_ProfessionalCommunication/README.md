# TCM 34000: Professional Communication

Purdue University Indianapolis, Polytechnic Institute | Summer 2025

## About the Course

A writing course focused on workplace communication. Assignments covered planning and revising messages for a defined audience in realistic business scenarios.

## Topics

* Neutral and positive messages
* Persuasive messages
* Bad news messages
* Tone, structure, and revision

## Skills

Audience analysis, clear and concise writing, professional tone, revising for the reader
