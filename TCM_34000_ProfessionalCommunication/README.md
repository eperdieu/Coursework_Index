# TCM 34000: Professional Communication

Purdue University Indianapolis, Polytechnic Institute | Summer 2025

## About the Course

A writing course focused on workplace communication. Assignments covered planning and revising messages for a defined audience. All scenarios in this course were fictional, and the companies and people named in them are part of the exercises.

## Topics

* Neutral and positive messages
* Persuasive messages
* Bad news messages
* Tone, structure, and revision

## Skills

Audience analysis, clear and concise writing, professional tone, revising for the reader

## Sample Writing (fictional scenarios)

* Security_Procedures_Email.pdf: a neutral message announcing new ID card access rules to employees
* Persuasive_Message.pdf: a response to a dissatisfied hotel guest offering a make good
* Bad_News_Message.pdf: a memo explaining why an employee suggestion was not adopted
